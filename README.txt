PASSPORT MATCH — COUNTRIES & NATIONALITIES

To test locally:
1. Open index.html in your browser.

To publish on Netlify:
1. Upload this entire ZIP file to Netlify Drop or deploy the extracted folder.
2. Confirm that index.html is in the root of the uploaded folder.

Game rules:
- Choose a numbered picture card first.
- Choose a letter vocabulary card second.
- Correct match: +10 points and cards remain open.
- Incorrect match: cards turn face down again.
- Find all 15 pairs to win.
